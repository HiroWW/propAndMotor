# Image sources (wiring diagram)

Product photos are used to identify the parts in the internal wiring diagram; rights remain with their owners.

The reviewed diagram uses the assets listed below. The old generic E-stop, WAGO,
banana, AC-breaker and CP30-BA images are retained as historical assets but are no longer used.
Manual excerpts are reference material, not newly drawn or AI-generated circuit figures.
